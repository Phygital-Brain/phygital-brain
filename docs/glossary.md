# Glossary

Words you'll see in Phygital Brain, in plain terms.

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **About pages** | The notes in `About/`: you, your goals, your work, your customers and your voice. Your agent reads them to understand you. |
| **Agent** | The AI that does the work in your brain folder: reads, writes, files and links notes. Claude is one; Codex, Hermes Agent and OpenClaw are others. |
| **Backup** | A copy of the files your agent is about to change, saved in the hidden `.backups/` folder for 10 days. "Undo the last change" puts them back. |
| **Brain** | Your Phygital Brain folder and everything in it. |
| **Brain Keeper** | The helper that tidies your brain. It runs on its own every work day, and does a full check-up when you ask. |
| **Check-up** | The Brain Keeper's full look at your brain, when you say "check up my brain". It lists problems and suggested fixes, and changes nothing until you say yes. |
| **`CLAUDE.md`** | A rule file. There's one at the top of your brain with the main rules, and one in each folder with that folder's rules. Other agents read it through a small pointer file. |
| **`.claudeignore`** | A list of files and folders your agent doesn't open unless you ask for that exact file, like `Memory/finances/`. It's a rule, not a lock. |
| **Cloud drive** | A service that keeps your folder on your computer and online at the same time, like Google Drive, Nextcloud, Dropbox or OneDrive. |
| **Connected apps** | The apps your brain reads from, like Gmail and Google Calendar, listed in `Skills/connected-apps/connected-apps.md`. Always read only. |
| **Connector** | An add-on that lets your agent reach another app, like a cloud drive or a calendar. In Claude, you add connectors in Settings. |
| **Current truth** | The top part of an About or Relationships page: the facts as they are today. Older facts move into a dated list below. |
| **Daily note** | One note per day in `Daily/`, named by its date, like `2026-10-20.md`. It holds what happened that day. |
| **Decision note** | A note in `Memory/decisions/` that records what you chose, why, what you gave up, and the risk. |
| **Dispatch** | A Claude feature that lets you send a task from your phone to the Claude app on your computer. |
| **Form window** | A window inside the chat where you click choices, type short answers and drop files. The setup uses four. Claude can show them; other agents ask the same questions in chat. |
| **Info block** | The few lines between `---` marks at the top of a note, like `status: active` and `tags: [...]`. Your agent uses them to find and sort notes. They stay in English. |
| **Lead** | Someone who might become a client. Leads live in `Relationships/Clients/_Leads.md` until they send a real brief. |
| **Link** | A pointer from one note to another, written with double brackets, like `[[Marta]]`. Click it in Baalda to jump to that note. |
| **Markdown** | Plain text with light formatting: `#` for headings, `-` for lists, `**` for bold. Any text app can open it. Your notes are Markdown files (`.md`). |
| **MCP server** | A small program that lets an agent use another app, such as a Nextcloud drive. MCP stands for Model Context Protocol. |
| **Meeting prep** | A short brief before a call, from your notes. Say "prep me for" and a name. |
| **Morning brief** | A short plan for your day, from your notes: your three most important things, what you're waiting on, and one decision to make. |
| **Operating system (OS)** | What Phygital Brain is for your notes: rules, a file system, settings, a background helper and apps, run by your agent. |
| **Plugin** | A package that adds skills to Claude. Phygital Brain installs as a plugin. |
| **Project front page** | The `README.md` inside a project folder: what it is, where it stands and the next step. |
| **Queue** | `Skills/brain-keeper/queue.md`: the list of things only you can decide, and small fixes still waiting. |
| **Report** | What the Brain Keeper did on one run, in `Skills/brain-keeper/reports/`. Kept for 60 days. |
| **Routine** | A scheduled task that runs in your AI company's cloud instead of on your computer. In Claude, routines run the Brain Keeper with your computer off. |
| **Rule file** | See `CLAUDE.md`. |
| **Scheduled task** | A job your agent's app runs at a set time, like the Brain Keeper at 7:00. It runs only while the app is open and your computer is awake. |
| **Skill** | A set of instructions your agent follows for one job. Phygital Brain has two: the setup and the Brain Keeper. Your brain also gets the no-ai-slop skill. |
| **Thinking tools** | Three ways your agent uses your notes to think: challenge an idea, find patterns, find contradictions. |
| **Writing Check** | The step where your agent looks for AI-sounding writing before anyone else reads it, using the no-ai-slop skill. |
| **Your own rules** | Rules you add by correcting your agent. They live at the bottom of the main rule file, and upgrades never change them. |
