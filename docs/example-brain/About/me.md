Ana Costa is an illustrator in Lisbon who makes picture books and book covers for publishers and small brands. She works alone from a home studio and sells her work as a freelancer ([[services]]).

She's best at drawing animals and explaining ideas simply, and works fast under deadlines. She says no to gambling and tobacco clients. She focuses best in the late morning, and long calls drain her.

## How I want my agent to work

- Short and plain: a few short sentences, everyday words, no jargon.
- Short lists and examples.
- Language: English.
- Time zone: Europe/Lisbon.
- Work days: Monday to Friday. Best focus in the late morning.
