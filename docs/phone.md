# Your brain on your phone

Your brain lives in Google Drive, so you can reach it from your phone. This is where a second brain really pays off: on the bus, before a meeting, right after a call.

There are two ways, and they do different things:

| Way | What it's good for | Your computer |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Ask:** the Claude phone app, with Google Drive connected | Finding and reading what's in your brain | Can be off |
| **2. Do:** Dispatch, sending a task to your computer | Adding things, filing, updating notes, the Brain Keeper | Must be on, with the Claude app open |

Most people use both: **Ask** to look things up, **Do** to add things.

## Before you start

You need:

- Your brain folder **inside Google Drive** ([Getting started](getting-started.md), step 2).
- **Google Drive connected** to Claude ([Getting started](getting-started.md), step 4).
- The **Claude app** on your phone, from the App Store or Google Play, signed in to the same account.

Read [What you share with your AI](../SECURITY.md#what-you-share-with-your-ai) first. On your phone, the same rules apply.

## 1. Ask: look things up

Open the Claude app on your phone and start a chat. Make sure Google Drive is turned on for that chat (in the chat's tools or connectors menu).

Then ask about your brain. Say which folder, so it looks in the right place:

- "In my My Brain folder on Google Drive, what's the next step on the picture book?"
- "In my brain, what did Marta and I agree last week?"
- "In my brain, what are my open tasks?"
- "Read my About/me.md and remind me how I price a cover."
- "Find the note about the print shops in my brain."

**Good to know:**

- This way is best for **reading**. Changing notes from the phone this way may not work for every file, and it skips the brain's rules for filing. To add or change things, use **Do** below.
- The phone app doesn't read your brain's rules by itself. If an answer feels off, say: "First read CLAUDE.md in my brain folder."
- Your computer can be off. Claude reads straight from Google Drive.

## 2. Do: send a task to your computer

**Dispatch** lets you send a task from your phone to the Claude app on your computer. Your computer does the work in your brain folder, with all the rules, then tells you on your phone when it's done. It's like talking to your brain at your desk, from anywhere.

**Set it up once:**

1. On your computer, open the Claude desktop app and find **Dispatch**.
2. Pair your phone by scanning the QR code with the Claude phone app.
3. Make sure your brain folder is one of the folders Claude can use.

Dispatch is new, and which plans have it can change. If you don't see it, check Claude's help pages.

**Then, from your phone, say things like:**

- "In my brain: I had a call with Marta, we moved the cover deadline to the 15th."
- "In my brain: new task, call the printer on Thursday."
- "In my brain: save this as a lead. A bakery in Lisbon wants a logo, found me on Instagram."
- "In my brain: give me my morning brief."
- "In my brain: run the brain keeper."

**Good to know:**

- Your computer must be **awake**, with the **Claude desktop app open**. If it's asleep, the task waits or fails.
- Start each task with "In my brain:" so it works in the right folder.
- Your agent still asks before it makes a new note, moves or deletes anything. You answer on your phone.

## Quick capture when you can't use either

No signal, or the computer is off? Write it down anywhere (your phone's notes app, a voice memo), then later, at your computer, paste it in: "Here's what happened today, file it."

## Reading your notes without asking

- **Google Drive app:** open your brain folder and tap a note. Notes are plain text files, so they open as text. Links won't be clickable.
- **Baalda:** there's no phone app yet. An iPhone app is planned.

## Safety on your phone

- Lock your phone with a code or your face. Anyone holding your unlocked phone can ask your brain anything.
- Your `Memory/finances/` folder is still off limits unless you ask for an exact file.
- If you lose your phone, sign out of Claude and Google from another device.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "I can't find that" in the phone app | Say the folder name: "In my My Brain folder on Google Drive...". Check Google Drive is turned on for the chat. |
| The phone app gives a vague answer | Say: "First read CLAUDE.md and About/me.md in my brain folder." |
| A Dispatch task never finishes | Wake your computer and open the Claude desktop app. |
| A note you added on the phone isn't in the brain | Use Dispatch for adding things. The phone app is best for reading. |
| A note shows up twice, one ending in `(1)` | It was changed in two places at once. At your computer, say "Merge these two notes." |
