# Use cases

The setup builds a different brain for different people. This page shows five of them: what the setup makes, what a normal day sounds like, and which parts help most. The people are made up; the folders, rules and helpers are the real ones.

**On this page:** [A freelancer](#a-freelancer) · [A small business owner](#a-small-business-owner) · [An employee](#an-employee) · [A student](#a-student) · [Personal projects](#personal-projects) · [By task](#by-task)

| Person | Sells things? | What the setup adds for them |
|---|---|---|
| A freelancer | Yes | Business, services and customer pages, a client folder with leads, writing in their voice |
| A small business owner | Yes | The same, plus a team folder and partner notes |
| An employee | No | Work projects, colleagues as collaborators, decisions with the reason |
| A student | No | Study projects, research notes, applications for internships and grants |
| Personal projects | No | Projects, ideas, a library of how-tos, a light morning brief |

## A freelancer

**Ana, illustrator.** She works alone for publishers and small brands. She picked "Freelancer or creator", "Yes, services or clients", and "Organise projects", "Remember people" and "Write for me".

**What the setup builds:** `About/` with `me.md`, `goals.md`, `business.md`, `services.md` (covers, picture books, prices), `customers.md` and `brand.md` (her voice). A project for each book she's working on. `Relationships/Clients/` with a note for each publisher, and `Collaborators/` for the writer she works with. A morning brief at 8:00 and the Brain Keeper at 7:00.

**A normal day:**

| She says | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| "Marta wants the cover by the 15th." | The book's project page, the publisher's note (last contact this month), today's daily note |
| "A bakery asked about a logo. Nothing firm yet." | One line under **New** in `Relationships/Clients/_Leads.md` |
| "Write a quote for the bakery, logo and three sizes." | A draft from `services.md` and her prices, in her voice, for her to send |
| "I'm raising my cover price to 400 from January." | A decision note with the reason, listed in her daily note until she closes it |

**What helps most:** the client notes (who she worked with, when, and for how much), the leads list so nobody gets forgotten, and drafts that already sound like her.

**Tip:** drop in your price list during the setup. Your agent fills `services.md` from it, and every quote after that uses the real numbers.

## A small business owner

**Rui, runs a bakery with two staff.** He sells to walk-in customers and to cafés. He picked "Small business owner", "Yes, products", and all four "help with" options.

**What the setup builds:** everything Ana gets, plus `Relationships/Team/` for his two staff and `Relationships/Partners/` for the flour supplier and the market where he has a stall. His key people from form 3 get their own notes. His brief comes at 6:00, because he starts early.

**A normal day:**

| He says | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| "Café Lua wants 40 croissants every Friday from next month." | The café's client note, a project for the new order, a task to confirm the price |
| "Joana can't work Saturdays in November." | Joana's note in `Team/` |
| "The supplier raised flour prices by 8%." | The supplier's note, and a decision note if he changes his own prices |
| "What did I agree with Café Lua last time?" | His agent reads the café's note and answers |

**What helps most:** one place for every café, supplier and staff member, so details don't live only in WhatsApp. Decisions with the reason, so he remembers why prices changed.

**Tip:** private numbers (bank details, tax ID) go in `Memory/finances/`, which your agent doesn't open unless you ask for an exact file.

## An employee

**Sara, marketing manager at a software company.** She picked "Employee or professional" and "No" to selling, then "Organise projects" and "Goals and decisions".

**What the setup builds:** `About/` with `me.md` and `goals.md` (her goal: lead the spring launch), but no business pages. A project for each campaign. Her manager and two colleagues as notes in `Relationships/Collaborators/`. `Memory/decisions/` for the calls her team makes.

**A normal day:**

| She says | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| "In today's meeting we moved the launch to April 3." | The launch project, a decision note with who decided and why, today's daily note |
| "Remind me to send the budget to Paulo on Friday." | A task in the launch project |
| "What's still open on the launch?" | Her agent reads the project's next steps and open decisions |
| "Write my weekly update for my manager." | A draft from this week's daily notes and project pages |

**What helps most:** decisions with the reason (useful when someone asks "why did we do that?" months later), and weekly updates written from what actually happened.

**Tip:** check your company's rules first. Many don't allow work information in a personal AI or a personal cloud drive. Your company may have an approved agent and drive you can use instead.

## A student

**Lia, master's student in biology.** She picked "Personal projects" and "No" to selling, then "Organise projects" and "Goals and decisions". In form 2 she chose "Learning or studies" as the area that matters most.

**What the setup builds:** `goals.md` (finish her thesis, find an internship), a project for her thesis and one for each course, her supervisor as a note in `Collaborators/`. `Memory/research/` for what she reads. No business pages.

**A normal day:**

| She says | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| "Here's a paper on coral bleaching. Pull out what matters for my thesis." | A research note in her thesis project, linked to the project page |
| "My supervisor wants chapter 2 by March." | The thesis project's next step, and her supervisor's note |
| "I'm applying to the Lisbon marine lab internship, deadline May 1." | `Memory/applications/lisbon-marine-lab/` with the deadline and what she sends |
| "What did I read about water temperature?" | Her agent searches her research notes and answers |

**What helps most:** research notes that link back to the thesis, deadlines in one place, and applications with everything she sent.

**Tip:** in form 1, pick how you take things in best (for example "Examples" or "Step by step"). Your agent explains things that way from then on.

## Personal projects

**Tomás, engineer who makes a podcast on weekends.** He doesn't want work in his brain, only his own projects. He picked "Personal projects", "No" to selling, and "Organise projects" and "Remember people".

**What the setup builds:** a project for the podcast and one for learning guitar. His co-host and two guests as notes in `Collaborators/`. `Library/` for his recording checklist. A short brief on Saturdays and Sundays only, the days he picked in form 3.

**A normal day:**

| He says | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| "Idea for an episode: how bridges are tested." | A note in the podcast project's `ideas/` folder |
| "Save my checklist for recording at home." | `Library/`, ready to reuse every episode |
| "Booked Inês as a guest for the 20th." | Inês's note and the podcast project's next step |
| "What episode ideas haven't I used?" | His agent reads the `ideas/` folder and lists them |

**What helps most:** ideas that don't get lost, checklists in one place, and a brain that only works on the days he does.

**Tip:** pick your real work days in form 3. The Brain Keeper and the morning brief run only on those days.

## By task

What people use their brain for most, whoever they are:

| Task | What you say | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Remember people | "I met João at the fair, he runs a small press." | A note in `Relationships/`, linked from today |
| Follow up on leads | "Who did I promise to get back to?" | Your agent reads `_Leads.md`, tasks and recent daily notes |
| Keep projects moving | "What's next on all my projects?" | Each project's "Status and next step" |
| Remember why | "Why did I stop doing logos?" | The decision note, with the reason |
| Research | "Compare three print shops in Lisbon." | A research note in `Memory/research/` |
| Write like you | "Write a reply to this email." | A draft in your voice, from `About/brand.md` |
| Reuse what works | "Save this prompt, I'll use it again." | `Library/` |
| Plan the day | "Morning brief." | Your 3 most important things, what you're waiting on, one decision |
| Keep it tidy | "Check up my brain." | A full list of problems and suggested fixes |
| On the go | "In my brain: what did Marta and I agree?" | Your agent's phone app reads it from your cloud drive |

More examples are on each folder's page in [The folders in your brain](folders/README.md).
