## You can

- Use, change and share the code and text, under the licence.
- Say true things about it: "built on Phygital Brain", "based on Phygital Brain", "a fork of Phygital Brain".
- Show the logo next to a link to this repository, unchanged, when you talk about the project.

## You can't, without written permission

- Sell, offer or name a product, service, course or setup as "Phygital Brain", or with a name that could be mixed up with it.
- Use the logo, or something close to it, for your own product, service or account.
- Suggest that your version is the official one, or that Phygital-Brain made, checked or supports it.

A changed version you share needs its own name and logo.

To ask for permission, open an issue on this repository.
