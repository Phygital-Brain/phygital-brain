## Mtunzaji wa Ubongo

Mtunzaji wa Ubongo anautunza ubongo huu kwa njia mbili. Faili zake ziko ndani ya `Skills/brain-keeper/`.

- **Kila siku, peke yake:** `Skills/brain-keeper/daily.md` inaendeshwa kwa ratiba. Marekebisho madogo salama, ripoti, na orodha ya mambo ambayo {Name} peke yake anaweza kuamua ndani ya `Skills/brain-keeper/queue.md`.
- **Ukaguzi, {Name} anapoomba:** "kagua ubongo wangu", "check up my brain", "panga ubongo wangu", "tidy my brain", au kauli kama hiyo kwa {Language}: soma `Skills/brain-keeper/check-up.md` na uifuate.
- "anzisha mtunzaji wa ubongo", "run the brain keeper", au kauli kama hiyo kwa {Language}: soma `Skills/brain-keeper/daily.md` na ufanye mzunguko mmoja sasa hivi.
- "anzisha mtunzaji wa ubongo mtandaoni", "run the brain keeper in the cloud", au kauli kama hiyo kwa {Language}: fuata Hatua ya 3, "In the cloud", ya ujuzi wa `brain-keeper` wa Phygital Brain.
- "simamisha mtunzaji wa ubongo", "pause the brain keeper", au kauli kama hiyo kwa {Language}: tengeneza `Skills/brain-keeper/PAUSED.md` yenye mstari mmoja: tarehe ya leo na "Amesimamishwa na {Name}". Thibitisha kwa mstari mmoja.
- "washa tena mtunzaji wa ubongo", "resume the brain keeper", au kauli kama hiyo kwa {Language}: futa `Skills/brain-keeper/PAUSED.md` (faili hii haihitaji ukaguzi wa viungo). Thibitisha kwa mstari mmoja.
- "mtunzaji wa ubongo amefanya nini", "what did the brain keeper do", au kauli kama hiyo kwa {Language}: soma ripoti za karibuni zaidi ndani ya `Skills/brain-keeper/reports/` na uzieleze kwa maneno rahisi. Onyesha chochote kisichotarajiwa.
- Ili kubadilisha kinachofanyika kila siku, {Name} anaomba kwenye chat. Hariri `daily.md` wakati huo tu.
- Ukaguzi unaruka `Skills/brain-keeper/reports/` na `queue.md` unapotafuta noti yatima.
