# Suggested apps

Apps you can connect to your agent so your brain can use them. A connected app gives your agent access to that app and what's in it; a skill (see [Suggested skills](../suggested-skills/README.md)) teaches your agent how to do a job. You choose the ones you want. Each page says what you need and how to connect it. The rules for every app: [Connect your apps](../../docs/connect-your-apps.md).

| App | What it does in your brain | Good for | What you need | Page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google Workspace | Reads your Gmail and Google Calendar, read only: who wrote to you and tomorrow's meetings go in your daily note | People who use Gmail or Google Calendar for work | A Google account and an agent that takes connectors | [google-workspace.md](google-workspace.md) |
| Canva | Makes posts, covers and presentations in your Canva account, in your brand, and resizes them for each platform. Comes with the Canvas Design skill, for quick posters and visuals as PNG or PDF | People who post on social media, or make flyers, covers or slides | A Canva account (free works) and an agent that takes connectors | [canva.md](canva.md) |

## For your agent

- Offered together with the suggested skills, in one list: see `../brain-setup/SKILL.md`, Step 4.1, and the rules in `../suggested-skills/README.md`.
- **Check first:** an app that already shows up as a connector in this chat is already connected. Don't offer to connect it; if the brain doesn't read from it yet, ask only whether it should (setup Step 3.7b). For a set of apps, like Google Workspace, offer only the parts not connected yet, by name.
- For each one they pick, follow its page, including anything it says comes with it (Canva comes with the Canvas Design skill; skip that if you already have it). Never connect anything without their click.

## Add an app to this list

One page per app, named in kebab-case (`google-workspace.md`), with the same sections as the others: what you need, check first, connect it, good to know. Then add a row to the table above, with a "Good for" line that says plainly who it helps.
